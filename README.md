# My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man 3 

**Spider-Man 3** is a *2007 superhero film* directed by Sam Raimi. It concludes the original trilogy starring Tobey Maguire as Peter Parker.

---

## Plot Overview

> "Whatever comes our way, whatever battle we have raging inside us, we always have a choice." — Peter Parker

In this installment, Peter Parker finally achieves balance between his devotion to Mary Jane and his duties as a superhero. However, a mysterious **black alien symbiote** bonds with his suit, amplifying his darkest traits and bringing out his ~~heroic~~ vengeful side.

## Why I Love This Movie

### Top 3 Memorable Moments (Ordered List)
1. The intense aerial battle between Peter and Harry Osborn.
2. The birth of **Sandman** in the particle physics test facility.
3. The iconic *Bully Maguire* street dance scene.

### Key Themes (Unordered List)
- Forgiveness and redemption
- The consequences of pride and revenge
- The struggle between good and evil within oneself
